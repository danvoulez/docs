This guide walks you through everything from requesting access to sending your first context-aware AI message. By the end, your AI tool will answer questions about your company's products, policies, and brand voice without you pasting a single line of background — because Company Brain will already have delivered it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request Early Access">
    Visit [company-brain.ai/waitlist](https://www.company-brain.ai/waitlist) and fill in your company details. Select the plan that best matches your team size and intended use — Free, Pro, or Team.

    <Note>
      Early Access is open now. Most applications are reviewed and approved within **1 business day**. You'll receive a confirmation email with your login link as soon as your workspace is ready.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Workspace">
    After approval, log in at [company-brain.ai/login](https://www.company-brain.ai/login). You'll be prompted to complete your company profile:

    - **Company name** — used to label your knowledge base and MCP endpoint
    - **Industry** — helps Company Brain suggest relevant fact categories to get you started
    - **Primary AI tools** — select the clients your team uses most (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Copilot) so we can surface the right setup guides
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Your First Company Facts">
    Navigate to the **Facts** dashboard and click **Add Fact**. A Company Fact is any piece of information you want your AI tools to know about your business. Start with the context you find yourself pasting most often.

    Here are three types of facts to add first:

    - **Product names** — e.g., *"Our flagship product is called Horizon. It is a project management platform for distributed engineering teams."*
    - **Brand voice** — e.g., *"Our tone is professional but approachable. We avoid jargon, use short sentences, and write in second person."*
    - **Policy** — e.g., *"Our standard payment terms are net-30 from the date of invoice."*

    <Tip>
      Start with **10–20 high-impact facts** — specifically the context you paste most often into AI chats. You can expand your knowledge base over time. A focused, accurate library beats a large, patchy one every time.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Your AI Tool">
    Navigate to **Settings → MCP Connector** to find your unique MCP endpoint URL. It looks like this:

    ```
    https://mcp.company-brain.ai/YOUR_ORG_ID
    ```

    Add this endpoint to your AI tool's configuration file. Replace `YOUR_ORG_ID` with the value shown in your dashboard.

    <CodeGroup>
      ```json Claude Desktop (claude_desktop_config.json)
      {
        "mcpServers": {
          "company-brain": {
            "url": "https://mcp.company-brain.ai/YOUR_ORG_ID",
            "transport": "http"
          }
        }
      }
      ```

      ```json Cursor (.cursor/mcp.json)
      {
        "mcpServers": {
          "company-brain": {
            "url": "https://mcp.company-brain.ai/YOUR_ORG_ID",
            "transport": "http"
          }
        }
      }
      ```
    </CodeGroup>

    Save the file and restart your AI tool to apply the change. You only need to do this once per client — all future sessions will pick up the endpoint automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test It">
    Open your AI tool and start a new session. Ask it something that requires knowledge of your Company Facts — for example:

    - *"What is our brand voice?"*
    - *"What are our standard payment terms?"*
    - *"Describe our flagship product."*

    Your AI tool should answer accurately using the facts you added in Step 3. If the response looks right, you're fully set up. If not, double-check that the MCP endpoint URL in your config matches the value in **Settings → MCP Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
