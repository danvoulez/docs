Welcome to Company Brain — the AI context layer that ends the copy-paste cycle for good. Instead of re-briefing ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot every time you start a new session, Company Brain stores your company's facts, brand voice, policies, and strategy in one place and delivers that context automatically to every AI tool your team uses. Set it up once, and every session starts with a fully informed AI.

## Get Up and Running

Follow these four steps to go from zero to a fully context-aware AI setup for your entire team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up for Early Access">
    Request your spot on the waitlist at [company-brain.ai/waitlist](https://www.company-brain.ai/waitlist). Choose the plan that fits your team and we'll get you approved fast.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your First Company Facts">
    Populate your knowledge base with the context you paste into AI tools most often — product names, brand voice guidelines, policies, and team info.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your MCP Endpoint">
    Grab your unique MCP endpoint URL from Settings and drop it into your AI tool's configuration file. One change, done once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Every AI Session with Full Company Context">
    Open Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or Copilot and ask anything. Your AI tool now knows your business before you type a single word.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Company Brain is currently in **Early Access**. Join the waitlist at [company-brain.ai/waitlist](https://www.company-brain.ai/waitlist) to claim your spot — approvals typically land within one business day.
</Note>
