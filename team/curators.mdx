Curators are the people responsible for the accuracy and quality of your Company Brain knowledge base. They review submitted facts, approve or request changes, and keep the library current. Every Company Brain workspace needs at least one Curator — they are the final line of quality control before any fact reaches your team's AI tools.

## What Curators Can Do

Curators have elevated permissions that give them full control over the knowledge base lifecycle:

- Review and approve or reject submitted facts
- Edit facts before approving them
- Publish approved facts to the team
- Archive outdated facts
- Submit their own facts without going through review
- Access the full version history of any fact

## Assigning a Curator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Go to **Settings > Team Members** in your Company Brain dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Team Member">
    Locate the team member you want to promote to Curator. Use the search bar if your team is large.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change Their Role">
    Click the role dropdown next to their name and select **Curator**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Assignment">
    The team member receives an email notification explaining their new responsibilities and linking them to the Curator guide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Curator Limits by Plan

The number of Curators you can assign depends on your plan.

| Plan | Curator Limit |
|------|--------------|
| Free | 1 Curator |
| Pro | Up to 5 Curators |
| Team | Up to 25 Curators |

<Note>
  The account owner always has full Curator access. On paid plans (Pro and Team), the account owner does **not** count toward the Curator limit — your full allocation is available for other team members.
</Note>

## Who Should Be a Curator?

The most effective knowledge bases assign Curator responsibilities by domain, matching each Curator to the area they know best. Here's a practical starting structure:

- **Brand & Communication** — Assign someone from Marketing. They own facts about brand voice, messaging guidelines, taglines, and tone.
- **Policies & Rules** — Assign someone from Legal or Finance. They own compliance facts, contract terms, and internal policies.
- **Products & Services** — Assign someone from Product or Sales. They own feature descriptions, pricing rules, and positioning.
- **Strategy** — Assign someone from Leadership. They own company mission, OKRs, and high-level strategic context.

<Tip>
  Smaller teams often start with a single Curator who owns all categories. That's completely fine — you can split responsibilities later as your team and knowledge base grow.
</Tip>

## Curator Best Practices

<Accordion title="Set a review SLA">
  Aim to review submitted facts within **48 hours** of submission. When contributors see their facts approved quickly, they stay motivated to keep contributing. A slow review queue discourages participation and lets your knowledge base go stale.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do a quarterly audit">
  Schedule a recurring task every quarter to scan your published facts and archive anything that's no longer accurate. Products change, policies update, and teams evolve — your knowledge base should reflect that. The archive keeps the record without surfacing outdated information to AI tools.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Use the weekly digest">
  Company Brain sends all team members a weekly digest summarising what's changed in the knowledge base. As a Curator, this is the easiest way to spot patterns — facts being updated frequently may signal a process that needs clarification, and clusters of new submissions often reveal knowledge gaps worth filling proactively.
</Accordion>
