Getting your team onto Company Brain takes about 15 minutes. Everyone gets full MCP access on every plan — there are no per-seat costs, so inviting your entire team costs nothing extra. This guide walks you through inviting team members, distributing the MCP endpoint, and using Company Brain as a living onboarding assistant for new hires.

## Inviting Team Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Invite Screen">
    Go to **Settings > Team Members > Invite** in your Company Brain dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Email Addresses">
    Type in one or more email addresses. For bulk invites, separate addresses with commas — you can invite your entire team in a single action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a Role">
    Choose a role for the invitees:
    - **Member** — can view published facts and draft new ones for Curator review.
    - **Curator** — can review, approve, edit, and publish facts. See the [Curators guide](/team/curators) to decide who should have this role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Invites">
    Click **Send Invites**. Each invitee receives an email with a personalised signup link. They'll be joined to your workspace as soon as they complete signup — no further action needed from you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing the MCP Endpoint

Your MCP endpoint URL is a single address shared by your entire team — there are no individual per-user endpoints. The easiest way to distribute it is to include it in your team's onboarding documentation, Notion wiki, or a pinned Slack message.

Find your endpoint URL at **Settings > Integrations > MCP Endpoint**.

Here's a Slack message template you can copy and adapt:

```text
Hey team! We're now using Company Brain to give our AI tools full company context. 
Add this MCP endpoint to your AI tool of choice:
https://mcp.company-brain.ai/YOUR_ORG_ID

Setup guides:
- Claude: https://docs.company-brain.ai/integrations/claude
- ChatGPT: https://docs.company-brain.ai/integrations/chatgpt
- Cursor: https://docs.company-brain.ai/integrations/cursor
- Copilot: https://docs.company-brain.ai/integrations/copilot
```

Replace `YOUR_ORG_ID` with the value shown in your dashboard. Each team member configures the endpoint once in their AI tool of choice — after that, every session automatically inherits your full company context.

## Using Company Brain for New Hire Onboarding

One of Company Brain's most powerful use cases is new hire onboarding. Once a new hire configures the MCP endpoint in their AI tool, they can ask natural language questions and get accurate, approved answers drawn directly from your knowledge base — on day one, without waiting for a colleague to have time to walk them through things.

Encourage new hires to start with questions like:

- *"What does our company do?"*
- *"What's our brand voice and tone?"*
- *"What are our standard contract terms?"*
- *"Who owns decisions about product roadmap?"*
- *"What tools do we use for project management?"*

Every answer comes from facts your team has already reviewed and approved — so new hires get consistent, reliable information rather than whatever happens to be in someone's memory that day.

<Tip>
  Think of Company Brain as a 24/7 onboarding assistant. New hires in different time zones, contractors starting on a weekend, or part-timers who need to ramp up gradually can all self-serve accurate company knowledge without blocking a colleague.
</Tip>

## Contractor Access

Contractors can use the same MCP endpoint as your full-time team. Because MCP access is tied to the endpoint URL rather than individual user accounts, there's nothing to provision — just share the endpoint URL with the contractor along with the relevant setup guide for their AI tool.

<Note>
  If you offboard a contractor and want to revoke their access, go to **Settings > Security** and rotate your MCP endpoint key. This immediately invalidates the old URL. Share the new endpoint URL with your active team members so they can update their AI tool configurations.
</Note>

## Encouraging Contributions

A knowledge base is only as good as the effort your team puts into keeping it current. Here are three practical ways to build a culture of contribution:

**Run monthly knowledge sprints.** Once a month, open a Slack thread or short meeting where anyone can nominate new facts to add. This lowers the activation energy — instead of expecting individuals to contribute on their own initiative, you create a shared ritual around it.

**Add Company Brain to your definition of done.** For every product launch, policy change, or major decision, include "update the relevant Company Facts" as a required step before marking the work complete. This ensures your knowledge base evolves in sync with your company, rather than falling behind.

**Celebrate great AI answers.** When a team member notices their AI tool gave an exceptionally useful, accurate response — one that traces back to a fact a colleague contributed — call it out. A quick "shoutout to [name] for adding that fact!" in Slack reinforces that contributions have real, visible impact and makes the knowledge base feel like a shared team asset rather than an admin chore.
