The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard for connecting AI tools to external context sources. Company Brain uses MCP to expose your curated Company Facts — product details, brand voice, policies, and strategy — through a single private endpoint. You configure that endpoint once per AI client, and every session automatically inherits your company context without any copy-pasting.

## How the MCP Connector Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your Company Facts are stored in Company Brain">
    Curators review and approve facts so only accurate, up-to-date knowledge reaches your AI tools. Facts with **Live** status are served through the MCP endpoint; drafts and pending-review facts are excluded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Company Brain exposes your private MCP endpoint">
    Each organisation gets a unique endpoint at `https://mcp.company-brain.ai/YOUR_ORG_ID`. Your API key is embedded in the URL, so no additional authentication configuration is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You configure your AI tool once">
    Paste your endpoint URL into your AI client's MCP or connector settings. This is a one-time step — you won't need to repeat it for future sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Every new session queries Company Brain automatically">
    When you start a conversation, your AI tool calls the MCP endpoint and retrieves the facts most relevant to your query before composing a response. Your company context is always present, silently, in the background.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finding Your MCP Endpoint

Open your Company Brain dashboard and go to **Settings > MCP Connector**. Your unique endpoint URL is displayed at the top of the page — copy it exactly as shown.

Your endpoint follows this format:

```text
https://mcp.company-brain.ai/YOUR_ORG_ID
```

Replace `YOUR_ORG_ID` with the identifier shown in your dashboard. The full URL including your org ID is what you paste into each AI tool during setup.

## Authentication

Your MCP endpoint URL contains your organisation's API key as part of the path — there is no separate token or header to configure. Paste the full URL from your dashboard into your AI client and authentication is handled automatically.

<Note>
  Keep your MCP endpoint URL private. It acts as both your identifier and your API key. Anyone with the URL can query your Company Facts, so treat it like a password — don't commit it to public repositories or share it in public channels.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="AI tool says it can't reach the MCP endpoint">
  Check that you copied the endpoint URL exactly as it appears in **Settings > MCP Connector**. A missing character or extra space is the most common cause. Re-copy the URL directly from the dashboard and update your AI client's configuration, then restart the client.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="AI isn't using my Company Facts">
  Your facts must have a status of **Live** before they are served through the MCP endpoint. Facts in **Draft** or **Pending Review** status are not included. Open the Company Facts section of your dashboard, filter by status, and confirm your key facts have been approved and published.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Only some of my facts are being used">
  The MCP endpoint returns the facts most semantically relevant to each query — it doesn't send your entire knowledge base in every request. If important information is consistently missing, add a more specific fact that covers that topic directly. More precise, focused facts surface more reliably than broad, general ones.
</Accordion>
