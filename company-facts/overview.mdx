Company Facts are the foundation of everything Company Brain does. Each fact is a discrete, curated piece of company knowledge — a product name, a pricing rule, a tone guideline, a strategic priority — that your AI tools can look up and apply when generating responses. Instead of pasting background context into every chat session, you define it once as a Company Fact, and Company Brain delivers it automatically to Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Cursor, and any MCP-compatible client your team uses.

## What Is a Company Fact?

A Company Fact is a short, focused statement of truth about your company. Each fact has four fields: a **title** that identifies it, **content** that expresses the knowledge, a **category** that organizes it, and a **status** that tracks where it is in the review process.

Keep each fact tightly scoped. One fact should answer one question. The more specific and self-contained a fact is, the more reliably AI tools can retrieve and apply it.

Here's what a Company Fact looks like:

```
Title:    Brand Voice
Category: Brand & Communication
Content:  Our tone is professional but approachable. We avoid jargon
          and write in plain language. We're confident without being
          arrogant. We use "we" and "you" to stay conversational.
Status:   Live
```

## Fact Categories

Company Brain organizes facts into four main categories. Assign each fact to the category that best describes the type of knowledge it encodes.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Products & Services" icon="box">
    What you sell, how it works, key features, packaging, and pricing. Use this category for anything a customer or prospect might ask about your offering.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Brand & Communication" icon="megaphone">
    Tone of voice, style guidelines, vocabulary preferences, and what to avoid. These facts shape how AI writes on your behalf.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Policies & Rules" icon="shield-check">
    Pricing thresholds, approval requirements, compliance obligations, refund terms, and standard operating procedures. Use this category to encode how your business operates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Strategy & Goals" icon="chart-line">
    Quarterly priorities, OKRs, target markets, and company direction. These facts help AI frame proposals, analyses, and recommendations in line with where your business is heading.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Fact Lifecycle

Every fact moves through a consistent review process before it reaches your AI tools. This ensures that only verified, approved knowledge is delivered to your team's sessions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Create a new fact and give it a title, category, and content. Drafts are visible only to you and your Curators — they haven't been delivered to AI tools yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for Review">
    Once you're happy with the content, submit the fact for Curator review. A notification goes to your designated Curators to let them know it's waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Curator Approves">
    A Curator reads the fact, optionally leaves comments or requests edits, and approves it. If the fact needs changes, it returns to Draft with feedback attached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live">
    Approved facts go Live and are immediately available across all MCP-connected AI tools for your entire team. No one needs to reconfigure their setup — the context appears automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Your Facts

The **Facts dashboard** is your central workspace for building and maintaining your knowledge base. From here you can:

- **Add a fact** — click "New Fact", fill in the title, category, and content, then save as a Draft or submit straight for review.
- **Edit a fact** — click any fact to open it. Edits to a Live fact move it back to Pending Review so a Curator can verify the change before it goes live again.
- **Archive a fact** — when information is no longer current, archive it rather than deleting it. Archived facts are preserved for reference but are no longer delivered to AI tools.
- **Search facts** — use the search bar at the top of the dashboard to find facts by keyword, title, or category.

<Tip>
  The search bar searches across both titles and content, so you can find a fact even if you don't remember what you named it. Try searching for a keyword from the fact's content — for example, "refund" or "net-30".
</Tip>

## Limits by Plan

Each plan includes a different number of active (non-archived) Company Facts. Choose the plan that matches the depth of context your team needs.

| Plan | Fact Limit | Curators |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Free | 50 facts | 1 |
| Pro | 2,000 facts | 5 |
| Team | 5,000 facts | 25 |

<Note>
  Archived facts do not count toward your plan limit. If you're approaching your limit, archiving outdated or superseded facts is a quick way to free up space without losing historical context.
</Note>
