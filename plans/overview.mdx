Company Brain charges per company, not per seat. Every plan includes unlimited team members who can connect their AI tools via the MCP connector and immediately benefit from your shared knowledge base. What scales with your plan is the number of Company Facts you can store and the number of Curators — the designated team members who review and approve facts before they go live.

## Upgrading and Downgrading

You can change your plan at any time from the dashboard. Upgrades are instant; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle so you keep access to your current plan's features until the period ends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Billing">
    In the Company Brain dashboard, click your organisation name in the top-left corner, then select **Settings > Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your desired plan">
    Click **Change Plan** and choose the plan you want to move to. You'll see a summary of what changes — including any differences in fact limits and Curator seats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    Review the confirmation screen and click **Confirm**. Upgrades take effect immediately and your new limits apply right away. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Downgrading from Pro or Team to the Free plan reduces your active fact limit to 50. If you currently have more than 50 live or pending facts, you must archive the excess facts before the downgrade takes effect — otherwise the downgrade will be blocked until your active fact count is within the Free plan limit.
</Warning>

## Cancellation

You can cancel your plan at any time from **Settings > Billing** — no need to contact support. Your plan remains active until the end of your current billing period, so you won't lose access mid-cycle. After cancellation, your Company Facts and workspace data are retained for 90 days in case you decide to reactivate. After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted. You can export your facts at any time from **Settings > Export** before deletion occurs.
