Here are answers to the most common questions about Company Brain. If you don't find what you're looking for, reach out via the chat widget in the dashboard or email [support@company-brain.ai](mailto:support@company-brain.ai) and the team will get back to you as soon as possible.

## Getting Started

<Accordion title="Do I need a paid plan to try Company Brain?">
  No. The Free plan gives you 50 Company Facts and full MCP access for your entire team at no cost. No credit card is required to sign up — just create your account and start adding facts straight away.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
  Most teams are up and running in under 30 minutes:

  - **~10 minutes** to add your first Company Facts
  - **~5 minutes** to configure your AI tool with your MCP endpoint
  - **~15 minutes** to share the setup guide with your team

  After that, every AI session your team runs automatically inherits your company context — no further configuration needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is Early Access?">
  Company Brain is currently available by invitation during its Early Access phase. You can join the waitlist at the Company Brain website, and approval is typically within one business day. Early Access gives you full product access while the team continues to add features ahead of general availability.
</Accordion>

## Company Facts

<Accordion title="What counts as a Company Fact?">
  A Company Fact is any discrete piece of company knowledge that you want AI tools to know about. Good examples include:

  - A product name and its one-line description
  - A brand voice guideline ("We never use jargon")
  - A pricing rule or policy
  - A quarterly goal or OKR
  - A team contact or role description

  Each fact should focus on one thing. Keeping facts focused makes them easier to review, update, and retrieve accurately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do archived facts count toward my plan limit?">
  No. Only active facts — those with a status of **Live** or **Pending Review** — count toward your plan's fact limit. Archiving a fact immediately frees up that slot, so archiving is the recommended way to stay within your limit without permanently deleting knowledge you may need later.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I import facts from an existing document?">
  Currently, facts must be added one at a time through the dashboard, or drafted automatically by the automated agents available on Pro and Team plans. Bulk import from documents such as PDFs, Notion pages, or Google Docs is on the roadmap. If this is important to you, mention it via the feedback button in the dashboard so the team can prioritise accordingly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my fact limit?">
  You won't lose any existing facts if you reach your plan's limit. However, you won't be able to add new facts until you either archive some existing ones to free up slots or upgrade to a plan with a higher limit. A banner in the dashboard will alert you when you're approaching your limit.
</Accordion>

## MCP & Integrations

<Accordion title="Does every team member need to configure the MCP endpoint?">
  Yes, each person configures their own AI tool once using your organisation's shared MCP endpoint URL. The process takes about two minutes per person, and the URL is the same for everyone on your team — you'll find it in **Settings > MCP**. After that one-time setup, every new AI session automatically receives your company context.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will this work with AI tools you didn't list?">
  Yes. Company Brain is built on the open Model Context Protocol (MCP) standard. Any AI client that supports MCP HTTP servers can connect to Company Brain — not just the tools listed in the documentation. If you're unsure whether your tool supports MCP, check its settings for an option to add an "MCP server" or "context server."
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Company Brain with multiple AI tools?">
  Yes. Configure your MCP endpoint in as many AI tools as you like — Claude, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or any other MCP-compatible client. There is no limit on the number of connected tools, and all of them draw from the same Company Facts in real time.
</Accordion>

## Privacy & Security

<Accordion title="Can other companies see my Company Facts?">
  No. Each Company Brain workspace is completely isolated from all others. Your facts are only accessible via your organisation's unique MCP endpoint, which is protected by your organisation's authentication key. No other organisation can query or view your data.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
  Company Brain stores all data in the EU. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard encryption. If you have specific compliance requirements, contact [support@company-brain.ai](mailto:support@company-brain.ai) for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export my Company Facts?">
  Yes. Go to **Settings > Export** at any time to download all your Company Facts as a JSON or CSV file. You own your data and can export it whenever you need — no need to contact support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
  After you cancel, your Company Facts and workspace data are retained for 90 days. During this window you can reactivate your account and pick up exactly where you left off. After 90 days, all data is permanently and irreversibly deleted. To avoid any data loss, export your facts from **Settings > Export** before the 90-day window closes.
</Accordion>
