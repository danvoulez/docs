Every new AI chat starts from zero. Before you can get a useful answer from Claude, ChatGPT, or Copilot, you have to paste in your brand guidelines, explain your product lineup, summarize your company policies, and hope you remembered everything. Company Brain solves this by maintaining a single, curated library of Company Facts that gets delivered automatically to your AI tools via the Model Context Protocol — so every session starts fully briefed, every time.

## The Problem

Without a shared context layer, teams hit the same friction repeatedly:

- **Re-briefing AI in every session.** Each new chat is a blank slate. You waste minutes copying the same background context before you can get to real work, and you risk forgetting something important every time.
- **Inconsistent AI output across team members.** When everyone pastes different context, the AI gives different answers. Marketing writes in one tone, support in another, and leadership gets something else entirely.
- **Slow new-hire ramp-up.** New team members don't know which context to give the AI, so their output drifts off-brand and off-policy until they've absorbed enough institutional knowledge to brief it properly.

## The Solution

Company Brain gives your team a shared, authoritative knowledge base — a curated library of Company Facts that covers everything an AI needs to know about your business. Curators on your team review and approve each fact before it goes live, so the context your AI tools receive is always accurate, on-brand, and up to date.

Once you connect Company Brain's MCP endpoint to your AI tool of choice, context delivery is fully automatic. There's nothing to paste, nothing to remember, and nothing to maintain session by session. Your AI tools consult your knowledge base at the start of every chat and respond with the full weight of your company's context behind them.

## Key Concepts

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Company Facts" icon="database">
    The individual pieces of knowledge stored in your library — product names, pricing policies, brand voice guidelines, team structure, and anything else your AI tools need to know about your business.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MCP Connector" icon="plug">
    A single endpoint URL that implements the open Model Context Protocol standard. Configure it once in your AI client and every subsequent session automatically retrieves the context it needs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Curators" icon="users">
    Designated team members with the authority to review, approve, and publish Company Facts. Curators act as quality gates — nothing goes live until a Curator signs off on it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weekly Digest" icon="envelope">
    An automated summary of every change made to your knowledge base in the past week. Stay on top of what's been added, updated, or removed without logging in to check.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who Is Company Brain For?

Company Brain is the right fit if any of these describe your team:

- **Teams using AI tools daily** who are tired of re-pasting the same company background into every new session and want that context to just be there automatically.
- **Marketing and content teams** who need AI-generated output to stay consistently on-brand, using the right tone, the right product names, and the right messaging — regardless of who on the team is prompting.
- **Operations and support teams** who work from defined policies and need the AI to know those rules without manual briefing every time.
- **Companies onboarding new hires** who want new team members to get useful, on-brand AI output from day one — without needing weeks of context-gathering to brief the AI effectively.
